Nexvo Shorts

A private publishing tool for the Nexvo YouTube channel.

What it does

Nexvo Shorts helps the owner of the Nexvo channel produce short, original educational videos (YouTube Shorts) about science and interesting facts. Scripts, narration and editing are prepared on the owner's own computer, and every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published.

How it uses YouTube

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the tool uploads the approved videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads basic statistics (views, likes, watch time) for those videos. It does not access any other channel, and it is not offered to other users.
