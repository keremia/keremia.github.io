Privacy Policy

Nexvo Shorts · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Nexvo Shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the Nexvo YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos to that owner's YouTube channel and to read statistics for those videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). It accesses no other Google data and no other channels.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • To show the owner how their published videos perform.

Storage and sharing

Access credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. Video statistics are stored on the same computer for the owner's own use.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
